Brian Fox argues that as AI changes how vulnerabilities are found, security policy must address the full cycle of repair, requiring coordination among model developers, security vendors, and the open source distribution infrastructure including maintainers, package managers, and registries. The piece contends that faster vulnerability discovery by AI tools without equivalent remediation capacity will create a growing gap in the software supply chain.
